## Design Context

### Users
Portfolio visitors and curious market explorers. They should understand the idea and draw a first pattern without an account or explanation from the creator.

### Brand Personality
Considered, curious, approachable. A small creative instrument with truthful financial context.

### Aesthetic Direction
User-selected minimal creative tool: warm paper, dark ink, restrained terracotta, generous drawing space. Editorial serif headline with precise sans-serif controls. No market-terminal density, neon, decorative statistics, or invented results.

### Design Principles
- The drawing is the hero and the primary input.
- Motion explains how the sketch relates to observed history.
- Source status and historical dates are honest and visible.
- Keep useful controls accessible on touch and with a keyboard.
- Default to a quiet, light canvas with purposeful contrast.

### Portfolio presentation
The creator wants this to feel like a fun personal project that demonstrates product and engineering skills. Credit Jarrod Savard explicitly and link to https://www.jarrodsavard.com/. Keep technical implementation details in the optional creator section, outside the main drawing flow. Preserve the drawing experience and the warm editorial direction.
